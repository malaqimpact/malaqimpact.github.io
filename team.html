Founder and Chair

Arabiremu Carolyne

Arabiremu Carolyne is the Founder and Chair of Malaq Impact Initiative, which she established to build safer and more inclusive digital spaces for women and girls in Uganda.

She holds a degree in Mass Communication and is a professional photographer. As a trained communicator and visual storyteller, she leads Malaq's work to prevent and respond to technology-facilitated gender-based violence.

What guides us

Our values

01

Survivor-centred

Every response starts with the safety, dignity and choices of the survivor.

02

Evidence-informed

Our programmes and advocacy are grounded in research and documented experience.

03

Inclusive

We design our work to reach everyone, including those most often left out of digital spaces.

04

Accountable

We are transparent about what we do and answerable to the people and communities we serve.

05

Collaborative

We work with communities, government and technology companies, not around them.

06

Rights-based

We anchor our work in every person's right to a safe, free and equal digital life.
